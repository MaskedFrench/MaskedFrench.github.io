    The idea

    One job. Two attempts. One signature.

    No new process to learn. The same rule applies to every change: nobody marks their own work. The second attempt is not a second opinion — it is a second build, made without seeing the first.

    01

    An AI does the work

    It gets the job and the rules, and writes its own tests — the checks it thinks prove the work is correct. On its own, this looks finished.

    02

    A second AI rebuilds it, blind

    Same rules, fresh start, no access to the first attempt. If the rules can be read two ways, the two attempts will not match — and that is the signal.

    03

    A person signs the difference

    The disagreements become a short list, each tied to the rule it touches. A name goes against it, with the time it took. Under ten seconds is flagged.
