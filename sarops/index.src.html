Assurance

Verification and validation

Two different questions, answered separately. Verification asks whether the model solves its own equations correctly — a mathematics question, answered here by executable checks. Validation asks whether those equations describe the world well enough for the decision — a question answered only by comparison with external evidence. This page shows both, and names what has not been done.

Verification“Solving the equations right.” Code and solution verification against analytic cases, conservation laws and convergence.
Validation“Solving the right equations.” Comparison of model outputs and coefficients against published field data and accepted practice.
CredibilityThe evidence package a duty officer needs before trusting a number: checks, comparisons, limitations, references.

Framework follows ASME V&V 20-2009 and NASA-STD-7009A; the distinction is Roache’s and is carried into both standards.

2 · Validation — what the model is checked against

external evidence, per claim
Claim in the modelExternal evidenceStatus
Drift is leeway plus current, with divergence Allen & Plourde (1999) leeway model and taxonomy: downwind/crosswind coefficients per object class, divergence about the downwind direction. Method agreement
Ensemble spread grows with time and wind Breivik & Allen (2008) operational drift model: stochastic leeway perturbations, ensemble spread. Method agreement
POS from cell probability × detection Optimal search theory (Stone 1975); SAROPS operational practice (Kratzke, Stone & Frost 2010). Method agreement
Coverage C = W / S drives POD IAMSAR Manual Vol. II: coverage factor for parallel sweep, C = W/S; POD = 1 − exp(−C) for random search. Standard practice
Default sweep widths are plausible Koester et al. (2014): effective sweep width ≈ 1.8 × detection range (high visibility), 1.6 × (medium), 1.1 × (low). Order of magnitude
Posterior update after a search Bayesian update p ← p(1−POD), renormalised — the update used in SAROPS (Kratzke et al. 2010) and classical Bayesian search. Method agreement
The plan is operationally sufficient No trial, no measured POD, no replay of a real case in this build. Not validated
Sweep widths are correct for these assets Defaults only; real values require trial data per sensor, sea state and visibility. Not validated

What would close the gap

  1. Replay a documented case. Run a past search with the forecast that was actually available, and compare the first detection point with the planned boxes. Publish the miss distance, not the hit.
  2. Measure POD. Instrumented exercise sorties with known targets give detection rates per asset, sea state and visibility — the only honest source of sweep width.
  3. Blind comparison against SAROPS. Same scenario, same environment, two implementations; report divergence in centroid, containment and POS.
  4. Independent replication. A second team re-implements the checks from the specification and publishes whether they pass.

3 · Limitations, stated on the artefact

not a certified tool
  • No environmental feed. Wind and current are officer inputs; no EDS, no forecast interpolation, no sea state.
  • No certified coefficients. The leeway table is representative and editable; it is not the Allen & Plourde table reproduced in full.
  • No measured sweep widths. Defaults are order-of-magnitude values, labelled as such, not trial results.
  • Single datum, one object. No multiple datum scenarios, no survivors in different drift classes in one field.
  • No operational validation. Nothing here has been tested against a live search; the plan is advisory and a duty officer owns the tasking.
