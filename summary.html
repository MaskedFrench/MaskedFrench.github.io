Northstar Trust The one-page version · RETRY-04

AI you can check

Two AIs check each other. A person signs.

Most AI mistakes do not look like mistakes — they look like finished work. So one AI writes the change, a second AI builds it again without seeing the first attempt, and the places where they disagree go to a person. Every step is fingerprinted, so the record can be checked later. This is one real change, end to end.

The problem

A single AI reviewing its own output is one opinion, twice. It reads with the same blind spots that produced the error, and it will usually say the work is fine. Once the session closes, nobody can show what was asked, what was produced, or who approved it.

A signature with nothing recorded behind it is a click, not a review.

What happens instead

1One AI writes it. It gets the job and the rules, and writes its own tests.
2A second AI rebuilds it blind. Same rules, fresh start, never sees the first attempt.
3A person signs the differences. Each disagreement is listed with the rule it touches. The button stays locked until every item is ticked.

What it found, in one row

AI #1’s own tests4/4 pass
AI #2’s tests against AI #15/8 pass
AI #2’s tests against itself8/8 pass
Must be fixed before shipping2 mistakes

One AI alone would have reported 4 out of 4 and called it finished. The two mistakes: retries come back too early when the payment system asks for a longer wait, and a retried payment could be taken twice because a new tracking code is invented on each try.

What the record proves

  • ✓Two attempts exist, and neither AI saw the other’s work.
  • ✓Each disagreement points at a rule and a test that actually failed.
  • ✓A named person saw every item before signing.
  • ✓Nothing has changed since — re-computable by anyone.

What it does not prove

  • ×That the rules themselves are right or complete.
  • ×That it works in production — no live service was contacted.
  • ×That the signer read carefully. Only that they were shown it, and how long it took.
  • ×That two AIs agreeing means they are correct. It means they agree.
